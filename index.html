Good to know

Questions, simply answered.

What is a stem cell donor registry?

A registry keeps information about potential donors so that patients can be searched against eligible donor profiles when a transplant is needed.

How is a donor match found?

Matching uses genetic markers called human leukocyte antigens, or HLA. A patient's medical team and registry search for suitable potential donors.

What happens if I am identified as a potential match?

You would be contacted and provided with more information. Further testing and a health assessment are performed before any donation takes place.

Is donating generally safe?

For eligible donors, stem cell donation is generally considered safe. Medical screening and safeguards are used, although temporary side effects can occur and donation is not completely risk-free.

Can anyone join?

Registry eligibility requirements vary by organization, including factors such as age, health and location. Check the requirements of a legitimate registry before registering.
